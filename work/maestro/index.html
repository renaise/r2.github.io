Maestro

Programming + membership in one product

Role
Design Lead
Year
2026
Medium
Interface

I led design on Maestro: one product where institution staff, from zoos to orchestras, run their programming and memberships.

Problem

Staff needed programming and memberships in one place, with their records linked to what members see.

Process

I set the CMS foundation and introduced a review flow: the team checks numbered revisions in the Lab before the client sees one build on the Stage.

Frontier stack

Two patterns adapted for Maestro, plus a CMS workflow to test.

Institutional stack audit

Reviewed as benchmarks. Neither tool is part of the prototype.

Solution

Membership sits in one place: tonight's update and renewal dates on Home, held tickets apart from discovery in Events, admission in the pass. The admin portal compares revenue with the previous period.

Impact

Two prototypes and an identity, on simulated integrations and demo data. Next: test real staff tasks for completion and errors.
