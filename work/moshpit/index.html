Moshpit

Turning saved media into shared collections

Role
Designer + Developer
Year
2026
Medium
Product

Turns your Instagram download into collections others can browse.

Problem

Instagram's picture of your taste is visible to no one else.

Process

I built it on Fielder: a build script reskins Fielder's source, and both share one database.

Solution

Your download is read in the browser, and its topics become collections, shared by link.
