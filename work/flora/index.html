FLORA

A node canvas for AI image + video models

Role
Founding Designer
Year
2024
Medium
Product

I was Flora's founding designer: node-based creative workflows in the browser.

Problem

ComfyUI gives professionals control, but you download it, bring your own GPU and learn a UI built for engineers.

Process

I made the node the unit, so each AI art technique that goes open source becomes one more node.

Solution

Flora runs in the browser like Figma and supplies the GPU. Text, image, video, upscale and realtime drawing are all nodes. Fauna Studio takes it to agents.

Impact

Live at flora.ai with 50+ models, used by Nike, Pentagram and Lionsgate. We took live generation to BLACK BOX 001, Artifice's NYFW show in SoHo, February 9, 2024.
