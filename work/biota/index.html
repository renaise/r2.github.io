Biota

Imagine anything, seed everything

Role
Designer
Year
2025
Medium
Product

I designed Biota, an iPhone and iPad app that remixes templates with your photos and words into new images and video.

Problem

AI images + video start from a blank prompt. Most people would rather start from something that works.

Process

I made the template the unit: a recipe someone listed, with slots for your media. Remix opens it, you add a photo or an instruction, and Imagine runs it.

Solution

Home, Create and Profile hold ten screens and seven sheets. Each run spends Water, priced on the Imagine button. New templates wait for review before Home, and every prompt, photo and result is checked before it shows.

Impact

I coded the M1 TestFlight build from Figma as the public prototype, with its own identity and design system. The iOS app is in App Store prep, not released.
