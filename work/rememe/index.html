rememe

A creation-first social app for remixing

Role
Product Designer
Year
2023
Medium
iPhone app

An iPhone app where any post could be remixed.

Process

I designed a composable canvas: images, text and drawings stack, and remixing stays as fast as posting.

Impact

On the App Store from April 2022. In July 2023 it dropped its social features and became a meme maker.
