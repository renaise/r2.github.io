Artifice Brand Identity

The identity for Artifice NYC

Role
Creative Director
Year
2026
Medium
Identity

Artifice NYC is a curatorial research nonprofit. Its guidelines are published as a web page.

Problem

Three programs run at once, by different people, on deadline. It showed in overstated copy and extra buttons.

Process

I wrote a working manual in nine chapters, live at brand.artificenyc.org.

Solution

Every show is WHITEBOX or BLACKBOX, lab or stage, a hexagon or an asterisk. Carbon, chalk, cobalt for links.

Impact

The marks, tokens and files sit next to the rules for using them.
