The Artifact Index

An archive of creative process

Role
Creative Director
Year
2026
Medium
Product

A public archive linking each artifact to its people, materials + revisions.

Problem

Archives keep the finished object. The process, the part others learn from, rarely gets recorded.

Process

I started with the schema: artifacts, makers, materials and revisions as linked records, with consent required.

Solution

A free, citable dataset with provenance on every record and a stable URL per artifact.

Impact

Live under Artifice NYC, with the first events indexed.
