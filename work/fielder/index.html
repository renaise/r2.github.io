Fielder

Browsing images by likeness + color

Role
Designer + Developer
Year
2026
Medium
Product

An image library that arranges collections by likeness, color or date, in the browser.

Problem

Visual research means seeing many images at once. Folders show one, and sorting tools keep your files.

Process

I made it local-first: CLIP runs in the browser, and the library stays on your machine until you publish.

Solution

Three views of one collection: Similar, Color, Recent. Search takes words, a color or an image.
