The Depictions of Original Sin

AI film screening with Flow x Artifice

Role
Creative Director
Year
2025
Medium
Generative film, 30 minutes

A six-part AI film that reads original sin as rupture.

Problem

Bosch painted original sin as desire cut loose. I wanted that reading without his imagery.

Process

Each stage below, from reading to the room.

Solution

Six parts, five minutes each, 30 minutes in all: temptation, rupture, loss. No figure from Bosch appears.

Impact

Shown at Sights by Artifice, Brooklyn, 6 December 2025. The film is on the Artifice channel.
