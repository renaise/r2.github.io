Vamos

Multiplayer itinerary planning for travel

Role
Design Director
Year
2026
Medium
Product

A trip planner for groups: ideas, map + schedule on one screen.

Problem

Group trips live in chats and notes, and someone still has to turn them into days.

Process

With Fictive Kin, I designed one working prototype, sign-in to sharing, desktop + phone.

Solution

Places pin by type. The itinerary runs as an agenda or a month. Collaborators show as cursors. Plan my day fills open time.
