Renaise / Public work

Osmosis

An identity for task-specific AI

Role
Brand Designer
Year
2025
Medium
Identity + Site

Osmosis is an AI company that builds task-specific models.

Problem

Osmosis described itself in training jargon. The identity had to explain it without that.

Process

I tested three directions and took ink + mist from Huang Gongwang's Fuchun Mountains: gradual absorption.

Solution

A mist gradient, two blues, Exposure, Neue Haas Grotesk + PX Grotesk. Short vertical marks frame and divide.

Impact

Live at osmosis.ai, across the deck, site and product.
