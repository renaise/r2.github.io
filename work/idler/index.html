Renaise / Public work

Idler

Identity + launch site for an AI research company

Role
Brand Designer
Year
2026
Medium
Identity + Site

Idler builds environments to train + test AI agents. I designed its identity and the site it launched with.

Problem

Researchers need to see where tasks came from and how results are graded before they trust them.

Process

I organized the site around that review: sourcing, verification + grading, with training and evaluation kept apart. The identity is light in a category of dark ones.

Solution

The mark is a cube seen down its diagonal. Six units in solid + open states build the rest, and the same units draw the leaderboards, skill profiles and cost charts on idler.ai. To draw with the units I built Vector, a browser tool: pick the units + their colors, set a motion, tune speed, hole, margin, burst + wander, then capture a still or export the animation.

Impact

Launched at idler.ai after 2 two-week sprints.
