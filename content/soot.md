## Overview

Creative work accumulates faster than filesystems can organize. SOOT is a
consumer-grade visual vector database for thousands of images, videos,
references and files, but the interaction primitives for a self-organizing
filesystem did not yet exist. Traditional digital asset management organizes
assets through folders and metadata; SOOT uses multimodal vector search to
organize them by what they contain. That landed the opportunity: how do you
browse a library that organizes itself?

## Selected outcomes

Defined the product grammar for mapping information visually: Spaces, Views,
search, focus, metadata, upload, selection and sharing. The system turns an
archive from a hierarchy of folders into a spatial field users can navigate,
reorganize and publish, making complex ML infrastructure behave as simply as
copy, paste, zoom and drag.
