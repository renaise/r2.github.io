Information

Renaise Kim (b. 1998) is a designer and curator working across culture + venture in consumer media, creative tools and deep tech. Founder of Codex Foundry, an applications platform. Creative director of Artifice NYC, a curatorial research nonprofit. Founder + creative director of Studio Artifice, a commercial studio. Her research is on artifice: the artifacts of speculative fiction and hybrid models of creation.
